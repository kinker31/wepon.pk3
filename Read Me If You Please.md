# Wepon.pk3: Kill Fast, when Bullets Too Slow!
The primary fires of most of your weapons should function more or less identical to their vanilla counterparts, but every weapon in this mod also has an altfire too! Rundowns on those, as well as any wepon completely reworked from the ground up, are as follows:

* **Your Fists**
    * Primary and Altfire control both of your hands, and your punching's now ambidexterious.
* **Twin-Bladed Chainsaw**
    * Attacks much faster, and benefits from Berserk!
    * Altfire spins you round and round whilst dealing heavy damage to anyone in the vicinity.
* **Pistol(s)**
    * Functions more like the vanilla chaingun, but can be toggled to fire infinitely for a reduced DPS.
    * Altfire uses UAC Technobabble Science Magic to spit out a diamond-shaped bullet pattern. Not as powerful as the chaingun, but much more stable.
    * Reload swaps between piercing boolet and normal boolet. Piercing boolets have more limited range, but has a higher DPS.
* **Shotgun**
    * Can be toggled between vanilla-esque functionality or an alternative, slugshot/buckshot style damage calculation.
    * Slugs pierce through up to five enemies, but are fewer in number, buckshot doesn't but is more numerous.
    * Altfire spits out a few mini-missiles, designed to deal with crowds when either you don't have the SSG yet... or you're in Doom 1.
    * Hold down Reload to pump shells into the gun.
* **Super Shotgun**
    * Shares the same damage logic as the Shotgun.
    * Primary fires two barrels, altfire twice that.
    * You still have to manually reload.
* **Chaingun**
    * Takes a long while to spin up, but when it does, it sprays everything in the living vicinity with many, many bullet.
    * (Thanks for letting me "borrow" your Doom Delta code, DrPsySpy!)
    * Altfire pre-spins the barrel, but still wakes up enemies.
* **Rocket Launcher**
    * Your rockets now deal splash damage to Boss Monsters, fire with reckless abaddon!
    * Unfortunately, since you're not from Space Scotland, you are still vulnerable to self-damage.
    * Altfire loads up the butt of the rocket with extra rockets, fire your weapon to barf them out.
    * You can swap between rockets and grenades using Reload.
* **Plasma Rifle**
    * Primary fire gradually spreads out in a fan formation as you fire the weapon.
    * Altfire poops out bouncing plasma mines that spit out yet more plasma bolts.
* **BFG9000**
    * Can to toggled to accept special BFG cells instead of normal plasma, saving your Plasma Rifle from the dustbin!
    * Primary's tracers pierce through all enemies for a long, long range.
    * Secondary barfs out many, MANY bolts.
* **ID24's Flamethrower**
    * Don't you love mods that require id24res.wad to function properly?
    * You take reduced self-damage when using the primary
    * Altfire spits out gobs of napalm that hurt enemies a lot.
* **ID24's Calamity Blade**
    * Primary's wave of fire is a lot stronger, and can mulch enemies better
    * Shares the same cell logic as the BFG
    * Don't be in a closed room if you choose to unleash the altfire. And have a ***LOT*** of ammo ready.

# Adonn.pk3: The Crap Wepon.pk3 Didn't Give You!
Most of the items should have good descriptions in the in-game menu, but here's a quick run-down on some of hte superwepons you'll find (if you have them turned on):

* The Golden Pistol Of Destiny
    * Has an extremely rare chance to spawn with the BFG
    * Does stupendous damage, but you only get three shots.
    * Altfire lets you swap out the hitscan for more projectile damage.
* Ultra Shotgun
    * Deals the most buckshot out of the shotguns
    * Ridiculously wide spread, and only fires horizontally.
* Ultra Chaingun
    * Slower to spin, faster to shoot, more damage I guess.
    * Reload spins the gun, altfire spews mini-rockets one you've fired it enough.
* Ultra Rocket Launcher
    * It's six weapons in one, actually! Cycle through 'em with altfire.
    * If you know how ROTT works, you'll know how this gun works.
    * Heatseekers don't consider torches to be valid targets now, that's nice!
* Ultra Plasma Rifle
    *  Primary fires a plasma yo-yo that you can charge to deal major damage
    * Altfire requires a lot of charging, but rewards you with a charging ball 'o plasma death.
